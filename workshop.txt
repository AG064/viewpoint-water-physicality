title=Water Physicality - Viewpoint
description=Water for sinks, bathtubs and showers: real 3D water with Project Viewpoint, and the same water painted into the base game's isometric view without it.
description=
description=[h2]What you get[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Sinks[/b] (kitchen, bathroom, pedestal, double and industrial sinks, and drinking fountains) get water and a tap stream while someone uses them: drinking, washing, filling a container. Each stream starts at the faucet's real spout; drinking fountains get an arcing jet from the bubbler.
description=[*] [b]Bathtubs[/b] get water that rises and drains, and a stream from the tub tap while it runs.
description=[*] [b]Showers[/b] get an animated spray from the shower head.
description=[*] [b]Isometric view[/b]: the water and streams are painted into the fixture's own art, so they sort and fade with it and never spill over the rim or show through walls and mirrors.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Two versions in this item: enable one[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Water Physicality - Viewpoint (3D + isometric)[/b] (Mod ID [b]WaterPhysicality[/b]): both kinds of water. Real 3D water and streams in Viewpoint's 3D view, and the painted water in the isometric view. Without Viewpoint it shows only the isometric water. Existing saves keep working with this one.
description=[*] [b]Water Physicality - Flat[/b] (Mod ID [b]WaterPhysicalityFlat[/b]): only the isometric water. Lighter: no 3D models and no Java part.
description=[*] They cannot be enabled together.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[b]None.[/b] Everything below is optional.
description=[list]
description=[*] For 3D water (Viewpoint version only): [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url]. ZombieBuddy asks you to approve the mod's small Java part on first launch.
description=[*] Recommended with Viewpoint: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3810302175]3D models for Viewpoint[/url] (sour_kisel). The water is measured from its fixture models, so it fits them exactly.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Works with[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3403870858]Lifestyle: Hobbies[/url]: water rises while the tub fills, stays while you bathe and drains afterwards, with Lifestyle's steam and bubbles drawn on top. The shower spray runs while you shower. Drinking or washing at a tub runs its tap.
description=[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3592172476]Take A Bath And Shower [SP/MP][/url]: tubs follow their real fill level and showers run. With both bath mods enabled, Take A Bath And Shower takes over bathing.
description=[*] Neither: sinks, tubs and showers show water and a stream while a normal water action (drinking, washing, filling a container) runs on them. Nothing fills up by itself, because the base game has no bathing.
description=[/list]
description=Both bath mods are detected at runtime: nothing to configure.
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] The server needs the mod: add Workshop ID [b]3827710289[/b] and the Mod ID of the version you use ([b]WaterPhysicality[/b] or [b]WaterPhysicalityFlat[/b]).
description=[*] When a player uses a fixture, a tiny server relay tells the players nearby, so everyone sees the same water. It stores nothing and changes nothing in the world.
description=[*] The water itself is drawn by each player's game and is never sent to the server.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Stand-alone wall taps and other fixtures are not covered yet.
description=[*] Tub variants added by Take A Bath And Shower use the standard tub's shape, so their water can sit slightly off.
description=[*] Light on performance: one scan per second for fixtures near you, and a small update ten times a second for the few in range. Nothing is drawn by Lua.
description=[/list]
