title=Viewpoint - Water Physicality
description=Water in 3D for sinks, bathtubs and showers, drawn by Project Viewpoint.
description=
description=[h2]What you get[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Bathtubs show real water that rises and drains with the tub's actual fill level. The waterline follows the tub's sloped walls.
description=[*] A stream runs from the tub tap while the tub fills.
description=[*] Sinks (kitchen, bathroom, pedestal and wall sinks) get water and a tap stream only while you use them: drinking, washing, filling containers. Nothing sits full all the time.
description=[*] Showers get an animated spray that starts at the shower head, for stand-alone showers and tubs with a shower.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Project Viewpoint
description=[*] ZombieBuddy (the mod ships a small Java part, so ZombieBuddy asks you to approve it on first launch)
description=[/list]
description=[h2]Recommended[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Take A Bath And Shower [SP/MP] or Lifestyle: Hobbies, for full functionality: they add bathing, so tubs fill and drain and showers run. With both enabled, Take A Bath And Shower takes over bathing (it hides Lifestyle's own bath and shower menu options) and the water follows it.
description=[*] Without either one the mod still works: sinks, tubs and showers show water and a stream while a normal water action (washing, filling a container, drinking) runs on them. Nothing fills up by itself, because the base game has no bathing.
description=[/list]
description=Recommended: "3D models for Viewpoint" (sour_kisel) - the water is measured from the fixture models in that pack, so it fits them exactly.
description=
description=[h2]Good to know[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Multiplayer: purely visual. The server must have the mod enabled (add its Workshop ID and the Mod ID WaterPhysicality to the server's mod list). Everyone sees the tub levels, the tub tap and other players' showers. A sink's tap stream is announced by the player using it through a tiny server relay (it only passes along a sink's position, nothing is stored), so every player sees it.
description=[*] Lifestyle: Hobbies (new, not yet tested by many players): its tubs and showers hold no water of their own, so the water shows while the bath or shower action runs (rising while the tub fills, full while you sit in it, draining after). Other players see it through the same server relay. Lifestyle's own steam overlay may show on top of the water.
description=[*] Stand-alone wall taps and other fixtures are not covered yet.
description=[*] Tub variants added by Take A Bath And Shower use the standard tub's shape, so their water can sit slightly off.
description=[*] The four Large Industrial sinks use a plain rectangle for their water.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Models and animation are generated, with a small throttled Lua scan (once per second) and no per-frame work.
